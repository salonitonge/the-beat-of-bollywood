Act I: The Duration Collapse

When Songs Were Melodic Epics

In the 1990s and early 2000s, tracks were patient narrative structures. Composers crafted 45-second live orchestral preludes with multiple distinct poetic antaras. With modern streaming payout algorithms and short-form reels, track length collapsed by nearly half.

Act V: Conversations Through Time

Seven Decades of Melody & Noise

A cross-generational listening session between Dadi (valve radios & acoustic orchestras) and Tara (streaming loops & AirPods).

1955–64
Dadi

"We used to gather around the valve radio every evening. Sixty musicians playing together in a single hall without any auto-tune. If someone made a mistake in minute four, the entire orchestra started the song over from scratch."

Jaane Kahan Mera Jigar Mohammed Rafi & Geeta Dutt • 1955
1965–74
Dadi

"Songs were written like ghazals back then. Lata ji’s voice carried genuine poetic longing across five full minutes. We were never in a rush to hit the chorus; the instrumental prelude took forty seconds just to set the mood."

Aaj Phir Jeene Ki Tamanna Lata Mangeshkar • 1965
1975–84
Tara

"Wait Dadi, this actually grooves! Pancham Da brought in that funky slap bass and brass section. Kishore Kumar sounds effortlessly cool, like he's winking right through my headphones."

Tum Aa Gaye Ho Noor Kishore Kumar & Lata Mangeshkar • 1975
1985–94
Dadi

"The electronic keyboards arrived, and every tea stall started playing these cassettes. The sound became softer and smoother, and Asha Bhosle's vocal intimacy felt as close as a whisper."

Roz Roz Aankhon Tale Asha Bhosle & Amit Kumar • 1987
1995–04
Tara

"This is where A.R. Rahman completely reinvented Indian sound. That deep digital bass layered right beneath Hariharan's Sufi vocal runs—it feels huge, spacious, and cinematic."

Tu Hi Re Hariharan • 1995
2005–14
Tara

"The iPod and ringtone era! KK's voice soaring over crisp acoustic guitar chords. No more lengthy instrumental introductions—the main vocal hook lands within ten seconds."

Aankhon Mein Teri KK • 2007
2015–19
Dadi

"People listen to short clips on their phones now, Tara. But when Shreya Ghoshal sings Kathak rhythms with Birju Maharaj, I still hear the spirit of our early mehfils. The song duration shrank, but the feeling stayed."

Mohe Rang Do Laal Shreya Ghoshal & Pt. Birju Maharaj • 2015
2020–25
Tara

"See Dadi? The intro was trimmed to four seconds, and it went viral on Reels before the film even released. But when Arijit holds that Indian classical alaap right in the bridge, the entire country still sings along."

Kesariya Arijit Singh • 2022
