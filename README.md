# The Beat of Bollywood: 70 Years of Hindi Cinema Sound

An website that shows how Bollywood music changed over 70 years (1950s to 2020s)—from 6-minute slow songs to quick 2.5-minute streaming hits.

**Live Website:** [the-beat-of-bollywood.vercel.app](https://the-beat-of-bollywood.vercel.app)

---

## The 6 Acts of the Story

* **Act 1: The Runtime Collapse**  
  Songs dropped from 6 minutes to under 3 minutes because of streaming apps, skip buttons, and shorter intros.

* **Act 2: The Lyrics Shift**  
  Word analysis of 6,600+ songs showing how old Urdu words (*ishq*, *nazar*) got replaced by modern words and English slang (*party*, *club*).

* **Act 3: Beat and Mood Changes**  
  Sound data showing how slow, acoustic instruments changed into fast beats, synthesizers, and party songs.

* **Act 4: Singer Monopoly vs Today**  
  How a few legends (like Lata Mangeshkar and Kishore Kumar) sang almost every song in the past, while today hundreds of singers share the space.

* **Act 5: Conversations Through Time**  
  How commercial hits started following the same formulas and chords over time, making many modern songs sound similar.

* **Act 6: How the Sound Changed**  
  An interactive theater visualization showing how the overall audio mix shifted over the decades—from soft acoustic melodies to heavy bass, loudness, and cinematic surround sound.

---

## Dataset Used

* **57,000+ Songs:** Master list covering almost every commercial Bollywood song made since 1950.
* **6,671 Full Lyrics:** Cleaned text files used to analyze language and word trends.
* **400 MB Audio Features:** Sound data measuring tempo, energy, and danceability.
* **Built With:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, D3.js charts, and hosted on Vercel.
